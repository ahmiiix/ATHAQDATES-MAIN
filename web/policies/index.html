Policies & Guidelines

Policy 01

Privacy Policy

At ATHAQ Dates Industry, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website or contacting our team.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery information, order details, and messages submitted through our contact channels.

How We Use Information

  • To process and manage customer orders.
  • To communicate about orders or enquiries.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To improve our products and website experience.
  • To maintain website security and prevent misuse.

Information Protection

We take reasonable administrative and technical steps to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Third-Party Services

Some website functions may rely on third-party services such as payment, hosting, analytics, communication, or delivery providers. Information shared with such providers is handled according to the relevant service requirements and applicable policies.

Policy 02

Terms & Conditions

By using the ATHAQ Dates Industry website, you agree to use the website responsibly and in accordance with these terms.

Website Use

Website content is provided for general information about ATHAQ products, services, and operations. Users should not misuse the website, attempt unauthorized access, or interfere with its normal operation.

Product Information

We aim to keep product descriptions, availability, packaging information, and prices accurate. However, product availability and other details may change without prior notice.

Orders

An order or enquiry may be subject to confirmation by ATHAQ Dates Industry. We reserve the right to correct obvious pricing or product information errors and to decline an order where necessary.

Intellectual Property

Website content, branding, logos, images, graphics, text, and other materials belonging to ATHAQ Dates Industry may not be copied, reproduced, or used commercially without permission.

Policy 03

Shipping & Delivery

We work to process and prepare confirmed orders carefully so that products reach customers in suitable condition.

Order Processing

Processing times may vary depending on product availability, order quantity, packaging requirements, destination, and other operational factors.

Delivery Times

Delivery estimates may vary by destination and courier. Any delivery timeframe provided at checkout or during order confirmation should be treated as an estimate rather than an absolute guarantee.

Delivery Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate delivery details, including the recipient name, phone number, and delivery address.

Delivery Delays

Delays may occur because of courier operations, weather, public holidays, incorrect delivery details, customs procedures, or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Policy 04

Returns & Refunds

Because dates are food products, returns and refunds may be subject to product condition, order type, and applicable requirements.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If an order arrives damaged, incorrect, or otherwise has a significant issue, please contact ATHAQ Dates Industry as soon as reasonably possible after delivery.

Order Review

We may request order details, photographs, delivery information, or other reasonable evidence to assess a return, replacement, or refund request.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the refund method and processing time may depend on the original payment method and the applicable payment provider.

Non-Returnable Situations

Products that have been improperly stored, opened, damaged after delivery, or otherwise handled contrary to product storage instructions may not qualify for return or refund.

Specific return and refund terms may vary depending on the order and applicable Saudi regulations.

Policy 05

Cookies Policy

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to help provide essential functionality and improve the browsing experience.

What Cookies Do

Cookies may remember certain preferences, support website functionality, maintain session information, or help us understand how visitors interact with the website.

Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow users to control or delete cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.
