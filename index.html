A Legacy of Quality

At ATHAQ, we don't just process dates; we honor a cultural icon. Our factory bridges the gap between the sacred traditions of the date palm and the rigorous demands of modern food science. Every date undergoes a meticulous journey of selection, cleaning, and packaging.

From the golden sands of our groves to your table, freshness and taste are the twin pillars of our production line. We utilize state-of-the-art cold storage and vacuum-sealing technologies to ensure that every bite is as vibrant as the day it was harvested.
